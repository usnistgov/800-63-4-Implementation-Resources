Digital Identity Risk Management Tool
=====================================

.. container:: coming-soon

   .. rst-class:: coming-soon-badge

   Coming soon

   .. rst-class:: coming-soon-title

   The DIRM Tool is on its way

   .. rst-class:: coming-soon-body

   We are building an interactive tool to walk organizations through the
   digital identity risk management process described in SP 800-63-4,
   from defining online services and assessing impacts to selecting and
   tailoring assurance levels. Check back here for the first release.

   .. rst-class:: coming-soon-body

   In the meantime, the full risk management process is described in
   `SP 800-63-4 <https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63.html>`__. You can also
   :doc:`submit a comment </comments/index>` to tell us what you would like the
   tool to do.
