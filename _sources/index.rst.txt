   A one-stop hub for organizations putting Revision 4 of the NIST Digital

In July 2025, NIST released the final version of SP 800-63 Revision 4. The
culmination of an almost four-year process that included foundational research,
two public drafts, and nearly 6,000 individual comments from the public,
Revision 4 responds to the changing digital landscape that has emerged since
the last major revision was published in 2017.

The four volumes
----------------

Web versions of all four volumes are available online, or on the NIST
Computer Security Resource Center site.
